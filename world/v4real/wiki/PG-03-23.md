# PG-03-23, Revision 7: Termination and Transfer

*(The wiki lists this page as Revision 6. The document itself is Revision 7.)*

## 1 Introduction

This guide gives direction to those responsible for modifying, disabling, or transferring access for terminated or
transferred Quillmark employees and contractors. A termination may be considered friendly (voluntary) or unfriendly
(involuntary). In either case, steps must be taken to ensure that access to company resources is removed.

A transfer occurs when an individual's job position or duties change, yet they maintain an employment relationship
with Quillmark.

When an individual terminates or transfers, management personnel, security personnel, and system administrators
must ensure that system and information access privileges are updated in accordance with the time frames identified
in Section 6.1 and Section 6.2. Initial responsibility lies with the individual's supervisor and the system security
owner.

This guide also documents procedures for regular review of access privileges to ensure that no one who has been
terminated or transferred inappropriately retains access.

## 3 Termination and Transfer Procedures

Access privileges must be modified or revoked within the timeframe specified in Section 6.1 for termination, or
Section 6.2 for transferring, whenever an individual's employment status changes, whether through friendly
termination, unfriendly termination, or transfer. Involuntary separations such as death, removal, and other similar
unscheduled separations will be addressed on a case-by-case basis by consulting People Ops.

Note: Disgruntled employees or contractors, whether or not they have been fired, can do significant resource-related
damage. Of particular concern are those employees or contractors who have resource administrative access (such as
system administrator, database administrator, telecom administrator, developers, etc.) and/or are in a significant
position of trust, including managers. Extra care should be taken by supervisors to ensure access privileges of
potentially disgruntled employees are removed within the timeframe specified above.

Note: For contractors, the contract sponsor is the contractor responsible for performing the procedures and
processes in the following sections.

### 3.1 Offboarding

When it has been determined that an employee will have a friendly termination or transfer the process described for
Offboarding Employees will be followed. The employee must complete the Pre-Exit Clearance Checklist. The
individual's supervisor has overall responsibility for certifying that all checklist items have been acceptably
completed prior to the final termination or transfer date.

### 3.2 Account Deactivation

Upon notification of a pending termination, the supervisor of the employee or contractor will initiate the
termination procedures by submitting a request to People Ops, who record the termination date on the HR record.
Accounts are deactivated automatically at the end of the recorded date. The request includes:
1) the name of the individual being terminated;
2) the expected departure date;
3) the name of the person who will be taking over responsibilities of the terminating person's documents and owned
   resources.
   Note: If no other person can be identified, the supervisor's name may go in this field.
4) a business justification.

### 3.3 Transfer Request

Upon notification of a pending transfer, the employee, contractor, or his/her supervisor will initiate the transfer
procedures by submitting a transfer request. People Ops records the transfer and its effective date.

### 3.4 Out Brief/Debriefing

The supervisor of the terminated or transferred personnel is responsible for ensuring that all applicable tasks are
completed prior to the individual's last official day in his/her current position. This includes ensuring that all
items issued by the company are returned to the issuing offices before the individual is transferred or terminated.
Individuals no longer having a working relationship with Quillmark must not remove company information.

### 3.5 Information and Resources

When an employee or contractor terminates or transfers, it is also important to protect the information and
resources they controlled. Ownership of groups, service accounts and shared folders must be transferred to a
custodian before the individual leaves. Transferring custodian responsibilities ensures security measures are
maintained in acceptable ways. Company information must not be moved to personal accounts.

### 3.6 Physical Access

The individual being terminated or transferred must be denied physical unescorted access to all facilities
following the out brief.

### 3.7 Special Considerations for Unfriendly Terminations

Unfriendly termination involves the removal of an individual under involuntary or adverse conditions. This may
include termination for cause, reduction in force (RIF), involuntary transfer, and situations with pending
grievances. People Ops coordinates with the Insider Threat Program regarding unfriendly terminations as necessary.

A termination of this type occurs only after the supervisor consults with People Ops. An evaluation of the
circumstances is conducted, including the reasons for the request, the supporting documentation, and potential
alternatives.

Disgruntled or upset employees must be removed from positions where serious damage to company property may occur
(including information and communication resources). In the event an employee must be removed or involuntarily
separated, or when an employee notifies an organization of a resignation and it can be reasonably expected that it is
on unfriendly terms, the following actions must be completed immediately:

● Deny access to resources. If an individual is terminated, resource access should be removed at the same time (or
just before) the individual is notified of dismissal.

● Relieve the individual of all duties. During the "notice of termination" period, it may be necessary to assign the
individual to a non-sensitive position.

● Require the return of all company equipment and information.

● Ensure the individual is escorted out of the facility.

### 3.8 Special Considerations for Contractors

Contract sponsors must ensure that assignment changes in contractor personnel will be communicated to IT security
personnel. In the event the individual terminated is a contractor, it is the responsibility of the contractor's
sponsor to notify the appropriate system security owner and to coordinate denying access.

## 4 Monthly Processes

### 4.1 Monthly Reviews (Employees)

Monthly reviews are done to ensure that the access privileges have been revoked for any employee who has been
terminated. Group owners are asked to confirm that no separated person remains in their groups.

Note: There is no corresponding monthly process for employees who transfer.

## 5 Annual Reviews

Annual reviews of user access privileges are required by POL-100 and PG-01-07. System Owners are required to review
user accounts to ensure that individuals who have accounts are not just currently employees or contractors but also
that access is appropriate based on their job functions and need-to-know.

Note: If the System Owner and Data Owner disagree on the level of authorization given to an individual, they will
consult with their management, and the system security owner if applicable, to determine the suitable level of
authorization.

## 6 Guidance for PS-04 and PS-05 Controls

### 6.1 PS-04 Personnel Termination

Control: Upon termination of individual employment:
a. Disable system access within [30 days of personnel termination];
b. Terminate or revoke any authenticators and credentials associated with the individual;
c. Conduct exit interviews that include a discussion of [privacy, disclosure, and confidentiality
   responsibilities];
d. Retrieve all security-related organizational system-related property; and
e. Retain access to organizational information and systems formerly controlled by terminated individual.

Common Control Implementation: For PS-04, account access is disabled within 24 hours of an approved request
indicating personnel termination. For unfriendly terminations, Section 3.7 applies instead.

### 6.2 PS-05 Personnel Transfer

Control:
a. Review and confirm ongoing operational need for current logical and physical access authorizations to systems
   and facilities when individuals are reassigned or transferred to other positions within the organization;
b. Initiate [modification of access authorization] within [14 days] following the formal transfer action;
c. Modify access authorization as needed to correspond with any changes in operational need due to reassignment or
   transfer; and
d. Notify [the new supervisor and data owners] within [14 days].
