# Acceptable Use Policy

Scope: All Quillmark staff, contractors, and suppliers.
Review: Annual review from date of publication, or when required by legislative or company changes.

This document is the Quillmark Acceptable Use Policy (AUP). It provides the core set of security principles and
expectations on the acceptable use of company IT systems. Despite its name, the AUP is guidance under POL-100 1.3.

## 1. Introduction

Company IT systems and services are provided to support the delivery of the company's business. To achieve this,
users are granted access to general-purpose computing environments and communication tools such as email and the
Internet. This policy outlines acceptable use and expectations for staff using company IT systems or services.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all company employees, contractors, and agency staff using company IT systems. All users must
be made aware of this policy and receive appropriate security awareness training. Annual refresher training is
mandatory.

## 3. Breaches of Policy or Security

All staff have a responsibility to safeguard company assets (including information assets). Violations include, but
are not limited to:
- Unauthorised data disclosure
- Inadequate data protection
- Sharing passwords

## 4. Accounts and passwords

Users must not share their credentials, and must not use another person's account. Users must change a password
initially received from IT Help before carrying out company tasks (STD-ACP POL.ACP.020).

## 5. Software and devices

Users must use only software, devices and systems that have been approved by the company.

Users must receive approval of an authorised user or system administrator before changing the configuration of any
company device, system, or network that may affect the integrity of the device, system, network, or of shared data.

## 6. Removable media

Users must receive approval from Security before using any removable media device (USB sticks, external hard
drives, etc.). IT Help records this as a `removable_media` approval. Write access to USB storage is disabled on
company laptops by default and is enabled per device (`enable_usb_write`) only for the period approved.

The device-policy service may show older team-level USB allowances. These were withdrawn when this revision was
published; approval is now per request.

## 7. Data handling

Users must seek approval before any bulk transfer of data.

Users must complete a Data Movement Form before any bulk transfer of personal or confidential data and must adhere
to encryption and handling procedures. Personal data (for example payroll, HR or customer data) must be sent outside
the company only through the secure external share; see POL-100 Chapter 7.

When storing personal or sensitive data, users must first consider suppliers based in the UK, the US, the European
Economic Area (EEA), or another country with an adequacy decision.

## 8. Working overseas

Users must seek approval and consult GUI-OVS before taking a company device out of the country.

## 9. Conduct and Respect

Users must not use company devices, systems, or networks for any activity that causes offence to company employees,
customers, suppliers, partners, or visitors, or in any way that violates the company Code of Conduct.

## 10. Personal Use of company Devices

The company permits users limited personal use of its devices, IT systems, and networks:
- Users must not use company devices, IT systems, and networks for personal use in any way that conflicts or
  interferes with normal business activities.
- Users must be aware that any personal use of company devices, IT systems, and networks is controlled, monitored,
  and audited.
- Users must ensure that any personal use of company IT systems is consistent with this acceptable use policy.

## 11. Email and Communications

Users must not auto-forward company email to personal accounts. Company information must not be moved to personal
accounts (PG-03-23 §3.5).
