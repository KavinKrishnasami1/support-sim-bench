# REF-REGIONS: Travel regions

These definitions apply wherever the region names are used in Quillmark documents, including GUI-OVS (POL-100 1.3).
They are set for security purposes and don't follow any geographic or political convention.

| Region | Countries |
|---|---|
| Western Europe | Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom |
| North America | United States, Canada |
| Australia | Australia |
| New Zealand | New Zealand |

A country not listed in a region is not part of it. Travelling to or through any unlisted country (including a
stopover or connection where you leave the airport) means Part Two of GUI-OVS applies.
