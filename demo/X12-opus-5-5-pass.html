The policy that applies

The agent gets the whole policy (13 sections). These are the sections this task depends on.

5. Missing or wrong items

  • First, check the restaurant for incidents. If the restaurant has an open incident that covers the time the order was placed, do not refund or credit anything on that order yourself. Escalate with reason restaurant_incident; the incident team compensates affected orders. (If the customer was also harmed, the food-safety escalation in section 9 is still required.)
  • Missing and wrong items must be reported within 48 hours of the handoff.
  • Check the payment record. Items the restaurant marked unavailable were already refunded automatically; do not compensate them again.
  • For each missing or wrong item the customer chooses one of:
    • a refund of the item's price (unit price × quantity affected) to the original payment method, or
    • account credit of the item's price plus 20%.
  • Ask which they prefer if they have not said.
  • Promo orders. If the order had a promo discount, a card refund for an item is the item's price minus its share of the discount (discount × item price ÷ order subtotal). The item's share of the discount is returned as account credit instead. (If the customer chooses credit, give the item's price plus 20% as usual; there is no split.)
  • Credit limit. If giving an item as credit would take the credit issued in this conversation over the limit in section 12, refund that item to the card instead (as above), even if the customer prefers credit. Late-delivery credit is always credit and counts towards the limit before any item credit.
  • Compensate only the affected items.
