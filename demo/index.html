support-sim-bench

A multi-turn benchmark for AI customer-support agents. An agent with tools and a written policy handles food-delivery support chats with a simulated customer, and each run is graded on the database it leaves behind, never on its wording.

Two runs, side by side

The same task, X12: a promo order with two missing items, where the customer wants one refunded to the card and the other as credit.
